A patient can make many appointments with one or more doctors in clinic, and a doctor 
can accept many appoinments with many patients. However, each appointment made 
with only one doctor , and each appointment references a single patient.  
 
Emergency cases do not require an appointment. However an emergency is entered 
into the appointment book as “uncsheduled for appointment management purposes.  
 
If Kept. an appointment yields a visit with the doctor specified in the appointment. The 
visit yields a diagnosis and , when appropriate , treatment.  
 
Each Visit updates the patients records to provide a medical history.  
 
Each patient visit creates a bill. Each Patient visit is billed by one doctor and each doctor 
can bill many patients.
